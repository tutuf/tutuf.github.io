# A small team of product engineers who think beyond the next release.

Since 2007, we have built, evolved and looked after software that businesses depend on. The people who ask the questions also shape the solution, build and test it, and stay accountable for what follows.

Each of us works across product thinking, software development and client conversations. Our different lenses make the work stronger; shared context keeps it continuous.

## Denitsa Belogusheva

*Co-founder · Engineering lead · 19 years in software*

Denitsa keeps the whole system in view: the business need, the technical consequences, the people doing the work, and the software that will live with the decision.

She has led work from the first client conversation to long-term care, and still writes and reviews code. When requirements are complex, she works directly with clients and partners.

She turns that same care into team practice through mentoring, feedback and reusable review workflows.

We work with only a few clients at a time, so senior attention stays close to the work.

Before an engagement begins, you know who will lead it, who else will contribute, and how much capacity is reserved for your work.

---

## Continuity is designed, not promised.

A small team should not depend on one irreplaceable person. We build continuity into the way the work is understood, recorded and owned.

1. **Decisions with reasons** — We record not only what was decided, but why—so future changes can respect the original trade-offs or revisit them deliberately.
2. **Knowledge that outlasts any one of us** — Automated tests record how the system should behave; technical and user documentation explain the rest.
3. **Yours from day one** — The code, repositories, documentation and access needed to run the system belong to you from the beginning.
4. **A visible continuity plan** — Before we start, we make clear how responsibility and context will be handled if someone’s availability—or the team itself—changes.

You should stay with tutuf because the partnership works—not because your software cannot leave with us.

We keep learning across product engineering, leadership and facilitation. We use the time AI-assisted development saves not only to build more, but to spend more on the judgment, edge cases and human consequences around what we build.
