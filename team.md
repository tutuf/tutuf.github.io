# A small team of product engineers who think beyond the next release.

Since 2007, we have built, evolved and looked after software that businesses depend on. The people who ask the questions also shape the solution, build and test it, and stay accountable for what follows.

Each of us works across product thinking, software development and client conversations. We each bring a different strength, and shared context means any of us can pick up where another left off.

## Denitsa Belogusheva

*Co-founder · Engineering lead · 19 years in software*

Denitsa keeps the whole system in view: the business need, the technical consequences, the people doing the work, and the software that will live with the decision.

She has led work from the first client conversation to long-term care, and still writes and reviews code. When requirements are complex, she works directly with clients and partners.

She looks past whether software works to whether the people using it understand it—and whether it makes their work easier. She shares that care with the team through mentoring, feedback and reusable review workflows.

**Focus:** Business discovery · Engineering leadership · Long-term delivery

## Sava Lachezarov

*Co-founder · Systems design · 25 years in software*

Sava thinks in systems. Having worked across product development, architecture, infrastructure and developer tooling, he looks at how the parts connect, where a change in one place will be felt in another, and which solution addresses the cause rather than the symptom.

He notices the friction others have learned to live with—a step repeated by hand, a detail people have to remember—and proposes the tool that takes it away, easing both the work and the mental load. He also removes code when the simpler system is the stronger one.

His background in journalism and facilitation shapes the way he asks questions, explains technical choices and navigates difficult conversations.

**Focus:** Systems architecture · Automation and tooling · Integrations

## Small by design. Specific about responsibility.

We work with only a few clients at a time, so senior attention stays close to the work. If we cannot give your work that attention, we tell you before we start—not halfway through.

Before an engagement begins, you know who will lead it, who else will contribute, and how much capacity is reserved for your work.

We are a remote company. We value autonomy and focused work, and we make time to meet in person because long partnerships need human connection too.

---

## Continuity is designed, not promised.

A small team should not depend on one irreplaceable person. We build continuity into the way the work is understood, recorded and owned.

1. **Decisions with reasons** — We record not only what was decided, but why, so future changes can respect the original trade-offs or revisit them deliberately.
2. **Knowledge that outlasts any one of us** — Automated tests record how the system should behave; technical and user documentation explain the rest.
3. **Yours from day one** — The code, repositories, documentation and access needed to run the system are yours, not held by us.
4. **A continuity plan, agreed up front** — Before we start, we make clear how responsibility and context will be handled if someone’s availability—or the team itself—changes.

You should stay with tutuf because the partnership works—not because your software cannot leave with us.
