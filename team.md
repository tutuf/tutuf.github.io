The people you meet are the people who do the thinking and the work.

---

## Continuity is designed, not promised.

A small team should not depend on one irreplaceable person. We build continuity into the way the work is understood, recorded and owned.

1. **Decisions with reasons** — We record not only what was decided, but why—so future changes can respect the original trade-offs or revisit them deliberately.
2. **Knowledge beyond the code** — Automated tests preserve how the system should behave. Technical and user documentation make that knowledge usable beyond implementation details.
3. **Yours from day one** — The code, repositories, documentation and access needed to run the system belong to you from the beginning.
4. **A visible continuity plan** — Before we start, we make clear how responsibility and context will be handled if someone’s availability—or the team itself—changes.

You should stay with tutuf because the partnership works—not because your software cannot leave with us.
