The craft of careful change

Not a clean slate.
A sound next step.

Every business already has a system. Sometimes it is software. Sometimes it is a spreadsheet, a manual handoff or knowledge held by one person. We learn what is valuable in it before deciding what should change—and what should become software.

ASK 01

What should become possible?

We begin with what the business, its customers or its team should be able to do differently—not with a feature.

ASK 02

What happens today instead?

We look for the real delay, risk, manual work or missed opportunity—and who carries it.

ASK 03

What already works and should stay?

We preserve useful products, processes and knowledge instead of replacing them with custom code.

You know the business. We bring the engineering judgment. The important decisions are made together.

A good fit?

We should probably talk if…

  • A process or system already matters to daily operations.
  • There is a real cost to leaving it as it is.
  • You want a partner who will question the solution, not just execute it.
PROBABLY NOT IF

A ready-made ERP, accounting or CRM product solves the need well, the product is still searching for product-market fit, or you need one supplier to cover an entire technology and design organisation.

A boundary we set for ourselves

Some work is not ours to take.

We do not work with companies whose business involves gambling, tobacco or weapons, or with companies built on exploitation, harmful addiction or serious environmental harm.

If a company asks us to build dark patterns, manipulate people or hide material information, we do not take work from that company—even when its industry would otherwise be a fit.

In our own work, we keep hardware in use for as long as it remains reliable and prefer durable systems over needless replacement.
