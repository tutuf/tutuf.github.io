## What has started to hold you back?

The right next step depends on how your business actually works, so we start with your situation - not with a solution. Most of our work begins in one of these four. Find the one that feels closest and open its questions to see it more clearly.

<div class="situation" markdown="1">

### A proven process has outgrown spreadsheets and workarounds

The business model works and your team knows how the process should run. But ready-made products do not fit the part specific to your business, and manual work is beginning to limit growth.

<details class="questions" markdown="1">
<summary>Before suggesting anything, we would ask…</summary>

* Which part of the process is already proven?
* Where does the work depend on a person, spreadsheet or workaround?
* What could the business do if that constraint disappeared?

## Four legitimate answers. Only one of them is “build”

Every line of custom code can create value - and becomes something that must be understood, changed and cared for.

1. **Use** Choose a proven product where it already solves the common need well.
2. **Connect** Make trusted systems work together around the actual business process.
3. {:.build} **Build** Create the focused custom layer specific to the way your business works.
4. **Remove** Take away code, steps and dependencies that no longer earn their place.
{: .answers role="list"}

AI made writing code cheaper. That makes the judgment behind what to write, what not to write and what to remove more valuable.

## We should probably talk if…

* A process or system already matters to your daily operations.
* Leaving it as it is has a real cost.
* Someone on your side knows the business and can make decisions with us.
* You want a partner who will question the solution, not just execute it.

### Probably not if…

* You want custom software where an affordable ready-made product already does the job. We would recommend the product instead.
* You need one supplier for all of your technology and design.

## A useful first conversation

**What is your business trying to make possible?** Tell us what you need, what is getting in the way, or what has become difficult to change. If one of the four situations sounds familiar, start there. You do not need a specification or a proposed solution.

1. **You write** A few lines about the situation are enough.
2. **We reply** One of the [product engineers](/team/) who would be involved reads and replies. If a conversation would be useful, we send you a link to choose a time. If we are not the right fit, we say so.
3. **We talk** A free, focused first conversation about the problem, the constraints and the best next step, even if that is not working with us.
