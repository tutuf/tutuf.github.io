Your company uses several different web apps to run its business. You struggle with synchronising the data between them and do a lot of manual work. Sometimes you make mistakes that cost you money or lost opportunities. It might happen that when you need to change something on the fly on your phone, the mobile app lacks the functionality or its web version would tell you it only works on a desktop.

You wish all the apps could seamlessly work, that your manual data labour could be automated, and you could.focus on the creative part of your business that brings the food to your table. We have the same problems as you and we decided to solve them in a way that would enable anyone to do the same for their specific business, be it producing organic food, creating hand crafted items of repurposed materials or something completely different.
