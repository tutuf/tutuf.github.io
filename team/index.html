DBPortrait

Denitsa Belogusheva

Product engineer · Engineering lead

Co-founder · 19+ years in software

Denitsa keeps the whole system in view: the business need, the technical consequences, the people doing the work, and the software that will live with the decision.

Over 19 years in software, she has led work from the first client conversation through prototyping, system design, implementation, testing, rollout and long-term care. She still writes and reviews code, improves delivery processes, automates recurring work and works directly with clients and partners when requirements are complex.

In a recent internal financial workflow, she kept working past technical completion until the interface made clear what the system had handled, what still needed attention and what the person could do next. She turns that same care into team practice through mentoring, feedback and reusable review workflows.

FocusBusiness discovery · Engineering leadership · Long-term delivery

SLPortrait

Sava Lachezarov

Product engineer · Systems design

Co-founder · 25 years in software

Sava looks for the general solution beneath the immediate request. Over 25 years in software, he has worked across product development, system architecture, infrastructure and developer tooling.

At tutuf, he shapes architecture, works with infrastructure and integration partners, and builds tools that remove recurring work across the system. He looks for the change that solves a class of problems rather than patching the same issue repeatedly—and removes code when the simpler system is the stronger one.

His background in journalism and facilitation also shapes the way he asks questions, explains technical choices and navigates difficult conversations.

FocusSystems architecture · Reusable capabilities · Infrastructure and integrations

Gabriela Velinova

Product engineer · Delivery and team practices

Gabriela turns incomplete requirements into coherent systems that people can use, test and support.

She designed and built the promotion-code system for a long-running e-commerce business. Continuity without dependency

Continuity is designed,
not promised.

A small team should not depend on one irreplaceable person. We build continuity into the way the work is understood, recorded and owned.

01

Decisions with reasons

We record not only what was decided, but why—so future changes can respect the original trade-offs or revisit them deliberately.

02

Knowledge beyond the code

Automated tests preserve how the system should behave. Technical and user documentation make that knowledge usable beyond implementation details.

03

Yours from day one

The code, repositories, documentation and access needed to run the system belong to you from the beginning.

04

A visible continuity plan

Before we start, we make clear how responsibility and context will be handled if someone’s availability—or the team itself—changes.

You should stay with tutuf because the partnership works—not because your software cannot leave with us.

Built together over time

Shared roots.
Room to grow.

We are graduates of the Faculty of Mathematics and Informatics at Sofia University. That shared foundation still shapes how we reason about systems, learn together and challenge one another’s decisions.

Beyond commissioned projects, we have mentored developers through initiatives including Rails Girls, contributed to open-source software and supported the local Ruby community.

We are a remote company. We value autonomy and focused work, and we make time to meet in person because long partnerships need human connection too.

We keep learning across product engineering, leadership, facilitation and AI-assisted development—then turn what proves useful into better ways of working.
